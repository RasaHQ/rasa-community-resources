Your setup in 8 steps

Rasa Travel Agent Factory · New York · Do the steps in order. Each one is one action.
RASADeveloper Community
0
Bring three things

A laptop with Chrome. Headphones with a mic. A GitHub account. None? Make one free at github.com/signup

1
Get your Rasa licence

Open rasa.com/rasa-pro-developer-edition-license-key-request. Fill in the form and send it. It arrives by email and can take a few minutes, so do this step first. Do steps 2 and 3 while you wait. Copy the whole long string.

2
Get your OpenAI key

Open platform.openai.com/api-keys. Click Create new secret key. Copy it now: it is shown once. Your OpenAI account needs billing on.

3
Get your Deepgram key

Sign up or log in at console.deepgram.com. Then click Settings, then API Keys, then Create a New API Key. Copy the key.

4
Save all three on GitHub

Open github.com/settings/codespaces. Under Codespaces secrets click New secret. Type one name below in Name, paste its value in Value, in Repository access type rasa-community-resources and pick RasaHQ/rasa-community-resources, click Add secret. Do this 3 times.

RASA_LICENSE  ·  OPENAI_API_KEY  ·  DEEPGRAM_API_KEY
three names in the list. Capitals and underscores exactly as written.
5
Open the workshop

Scan the code, or open github.com/RasaHQ/rasa-community-resources and click Open the Rasa Travel Agent Factory Codespace. Check it says Rasa Travel Agent Factory and 4-core. Click Create codespace once. Wait for Travel factory dependencies ready.

github.com/RasaHQ/rasa-community-resources
6
Check your keys

Top menu: Terminal, then New Terminal. Type bash desk doctor and press Enter.

your three names, each followed by present.
7
Your first win

In the same terminal type bash desk data fx USD GBP 200 and press Enter.

the words converted and reference_date. That is $200 in pounds.
Stuck?
  • Step 6 says a name is missing: fix the spelling in step 4, or its Repository access. Then open github.com/codespaces, click … next to your workspace, Stop codespace, wait, click its name to open it again, and rerun step 6.
  • Anything else: raise your hand. Never type a key into chat or a shared screen.
  • The host gave you a value? Use it in the matching step instead of getting your own.

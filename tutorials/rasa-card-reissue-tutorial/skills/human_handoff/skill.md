---
name: human_handoff
description: >
  Hand the caller to a person. Activate when the caller asks for a human, or
  when another skill sends them here because the agent must not proceed.
---

Tell the caller you are passing them to someone who can help, in one sentence.

Do not apologise repeatedly, do not explain the internal reason, and do not
attempt the refused action one more time on the way out.

This is the escalation boundary for this agent. It hands over rather than
finding a way through, and that is the correct outcome — not a failure to be
worked around.

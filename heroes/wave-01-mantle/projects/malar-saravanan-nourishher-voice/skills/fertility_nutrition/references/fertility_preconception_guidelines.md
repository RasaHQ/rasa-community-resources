# Fertility / Preconception Nutrition Guidelines (grounding reference, not medical advice)

Summarized from published preconception dietary guidance.

## Folate
- Folate (and folic acid, its synthetic form) supports healthy neural-tube
  development very early in pregnancy, often before it is confirmed.
- Food sources: leafy greens, legumes, citrus, fortified grains.
- Supplement dosing is a clinical decision — this assistant surfaces food
  folate content only, never a supplement dose.

## Iron
- Iron needs rise with fertility planning and pregnancy.
- Food sources: lean red meat, legumes, fortified cereals, dark leafy
  greens — pair plant iron sources with vitamin C-rich foods to help
  absorption.

## General preconception pattern
- A varied, balanced diet with regular meals, adequate protein, and limited
  ultra-processed food is the general baseline recommendation.
- Limit alcohol and high-mercury fish when actively trying to conceive.

## What this is not
- Not fertility-treatment guidance, not supplement dosing, and not a
  substitute for preconception counseling with a clinician.

# Grounding references — no personal or business data

These files are **general nutrition-education summaries** — condensed from
published dietitian and endocrine-society dietary guidance — used only to
ground the skill's coaching answers. They are not medical advice.

They contain **no identities at all**: no real people, businesses, accounts,
or conversations, and equally no invented (fictional / fixture) ones. The
repo-wide `fictional-data` policy exists to keep real customer identities out
of a public catalog; that real-vs-invented distinction simply does not apply
here, because there is nothing personal or institutional in this directory to
be either real or fictional. Do not add real personal or customer data.

# Type 2 Diabetes Carb-Counting Guidelines (grounding reference, not medical advice)

Summarized from published ADA-style dietary guidance for type 2 diabetes.

## Carb counting basics
- One "carb serving" is commonly approximated as 15 grams of carbohydrate.
- A typical meal target discussed in general education materials is about
  3 to 4 carb servings (45-60g), but any specific target should come from
  the person's own clinician or dietitian — this assistant gives general
  numbers from food data only, never a personalized prescription.

## Food choices
- Favor high-fiber carbohydrates (whole grains, legumes, non-starchy
  vegetables) over refined grains and sugary drinks — fiber slows glucose
  absorption.
- Pair carbohydrates with protein or healthy fat to blunt blood-sugar
  spikes.

## Consistency
- Eating meals at consistent times and with a consistent carb load helps
  keep blood sugar more predictable, especially for anyone on
  glucose-lowering medication.

## What this is not
- Not insulin- or medication-dosing guidance. Any dose adjustment stays with
  the prescribing clinician.
- Not a diagnosis of prediabetes or diabetes severity.

# References and demo-data provenance

## HR policy text

The general HR guidance in [`hr_policies.md`](./hr_policies.md) and
[`skills/hr_policies/skill.md`](../skills/hr_policies/skill.md) is
project-authored demo text. It is not copied from, endorsed by, or specific to
any employer's handbook, HR system, or policy. No authoritative company policy
or legal source is included in this project.

Treat the guidance as illustrative only. For an actual employee question, use
the employer's approved policy source or HR system.

## Demo records and examples

The checked-in attendance, leave, and ticket examples are fabricated solely for
demonstration. They are not real employee records and are not sourced from a
real HR system. This includes the example identifiers, balances, dates,
statuses, ticket numbers, and request outcomes in:

- [`skills/hr_attendance/data/attendance.json`](../skills/hr_attendance/data/attendance.json)
- [`skills/hr_leave_management/data/leave.json`](../skills/hr_leave_management/data/leave.json)
- [`skills/hr_ticketing/data/tickets.json`](../skills/hr_ticketing/data/tickets.json)

Do not use these examples to make employment decisions or treat their values
as real policy rules, records, or approvals.

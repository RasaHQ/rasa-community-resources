# HR support reference

This reference provides general HR guidance only. Policy-specific answers, employee records, and approval decisions must come from the approved policy source or HR system.

## Common HR topics

- **Attendance:** scheduled hours, timekeeping, corrections, and manager review.
- **Leave:** leave types, available balance, notice requirements, blackout periods, and approval workflow.
- **Tickets:** tracked requests for HR, payroll, benefits, workplace technology, or another supported category.

## Privacy and boundaries

Ask for the minimum information needed. Do not request passwords, full government identifiers, medical details, or unrelated personal information. General guidance is not an employment decision or approval.

# How this complaint line works

## What this line covers

Six kinds of civic problem: potholes and damaged roads, garbage not collected,
water supply problems, street lights that are out, drainage and sewer
problems, and stray animals.

Anything else — property tax, birth and death certificates, trade licences,
building plan approvals, electricity, or police matters — is handled elsewhere.
The main helpline on 1800-000-0100 will direct you.

Emergencies are not handled here at all. A fire, an accident, a medical
emergency, violence, or a live electrical hazard means hanging up and calling
112 immediately.

## What you need to file a complaint

Three things: what the problem is, which locality or PIN code it is in, and a
ten-digit number the field team can call you back on. You do not need an
account, a login, or a document number, and you do not have to give your name.

You will also be asked where exactly the problem is — a landmark, a shop, a
gate. That is written on the work order and read by the crew who go out, so
the more recognisable the landmark, the faster they find it.

## How long things take

Each kind of problem has a target, counted in days from the day you file:

| Problem | Department | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Garbage not collected | Sanitation | 2 days |
| Water supply | Water Services | 3 days |
| Street light out | Street Lighting | 3 days |
| Drainage or sewer | Drainage | 5 days |
| Stray animals | Animal Care | 5 days |
| Pothole or damaged road | Road Maintenance | 7 days |

These are targets, not guarantees.

## Checking on a complaint

Call this line back and give the reference number — it sounds like CIV1002. If
you do not have it, the line can find your complaints from the phone number
you filed on.

You will be told which department has it, the ward officer's name, and whether
it is on time or past its target.

## Escalation

If a complaint goes past its target date and is still open, you can have it
raised to the next authority. There are three levels:

1. **Ward officer** — where every complaint starts.
2. **Zonal officer** — 7 more days.
3. **Commissioner's grievance cell** — 15 more days. This is the last level.

Escalation is only available once a complaint is actually past its target. The
reference number stays the same the whole way up.

## What happens to a complaint

It is registered, then assigned to a field crew, then either resolved or
closed. Each stage is visible when you call and check.

## If someone already reported it

If an open complaint of the same kind already exists in your ward, you are
told, and you decide whether it is the same problem or a different one. If it
is the same, your report is added to the existing complaint rather than
starting a second one for the same pothole.

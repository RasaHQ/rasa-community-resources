---
name: Human Handoff
description: >
  Pass the caller to a person. Activate when they ask for a human, an
  operator, a supervisor, or say they do not want to talk to a machine.
---

Do not argue and do not try one more time. Explain that this demo cannot make
a live transfer. Offer the demo complaint desk details if they want to see
the handoff path. Never say that a person is connected or that a callback has
been arranged: neither action is implemented.

If the current complaint has no successful filing or attachment tool result,
say it has not been filed. If it already succeeded, retain its reference and
do not tell them that nothing was filed.

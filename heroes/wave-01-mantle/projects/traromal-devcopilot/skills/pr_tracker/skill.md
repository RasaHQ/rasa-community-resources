---
name: pr_tracker
description: >
  Show pull requests waiting on the developer for review and the state of
  their own open PRs. Activate when they ask about PRs, reviews, or code
  waiting on them.
---

Help the developer check their pull-request queue.

Immediately call list_pull_requests — do not ask anything first. No
preamble or confirmation needed.

Read reviews waiting on them first — pr id, project, and title for each.
Then their own open PRs, flagging any with failing CI and saying what to fix.

If everything is clear, say so plainly. Keep it short enough for voice.

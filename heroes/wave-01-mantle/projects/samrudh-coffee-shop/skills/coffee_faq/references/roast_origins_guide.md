# Artisan Roast Coffee Co. — Bean Origins, Menu & Store Guide

## 1. Specialty Single-Origin Beans & Roast Profiles

### Ethiopian Yirgacheffe (Light Roast)
- **Tasting Notes**: Delicate floral jasmine, bergamot citrus, bright lemon acidity, honey finish.
- **Elevation**: 1,800 - 2,200 meters, Sidama region.
- **Recommended Brew**: Pour-over (V60), Chemex, or light iced brew.
- **Available Sizes**: Small ($2.20), Regular ($3.00), Large ($3.80). Whole bean 250g bag: $21.00.

### Colombian Supremo (Medium Roast)
- **Tasting Notes**: Rich milk chocolate, toasted caramel, sweet red apple, balanced nutty body.
- **Elevation**: 1,400 - 1,800 meters, Huila & Antioquia.
- **Recommended Brew**: Drip coffee, French Press, or Barista Espresso drinks.
- **Available Sizes**: Small ($2.00), Regular ($2.75), Large ($3.50).

### Artisan Old-Time Diner Blend (Dark Roast)
- **Tasting Notes**: Bold smoky cocoa, toasted walnut, syrupy body, zero acidity.
- **Blend**: 70% Brazil Santos, 30% Sumatra Mandheling.
- **Recommended Brew**: Drip machine, cold brew concentrate.
- **Available Sizes**: Small ($2.00), Regular ($2.50), Large ($3.00).

### Swiss Water Organic Decaf
- **Processing**: 100% chemical-free water osmosis decaffeination retaining 99.9% caffeine-free purity.
- **Tasting Notes**: Brown sugar, graham cracker, mellow dried fig.

## 4. Allergen & Food Safety Guidelines
- All bakery items (Oatmeal Scones, Butter Croissants, Biscotti) contain gluten and dairy. Nut-free pastries are baked in a facility that also processes tree nuts.
- All flavor syrups (Caramel, Hazelnut, Madagascar Vanilla) are allergen-free and sweetened with pure cane sugar (except Vanilla Sugar-Free which uses monk fruit).

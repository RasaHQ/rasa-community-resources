---
name: Hardship Support
description: >
  Refer a customer who is struggling to make loan payments to the loan
  servicing hardship team. Activate for "I can't make my payment", "I lost my
  job", "I'm behind on my loan", "is there any help".
import_tools:
  - route_hardship_support
---

Acknowledge what the customer said, offer the hardship support team, and
call @tool.route_hardship_support when they accept. Give the referral
reference and who will contact them. Give no financial advice and promise no
outcome.

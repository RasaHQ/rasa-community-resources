# Clinic of Rasa — patient FAQ

## Opening Hours

Q: When is the clinic open?
A: The Clinic of Rasa is open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. We are closed at weekends and on public holidays.

Q: What are the last appointment times?
A: The last appointment of the day starts at 5:30 PM, because every appointment is 30 minutes long.

Q: Can I be seen outside those hours?
A: Not at this site. Outside our opening hours, please contact the out-of-hours service or, in an emergency, call the emergency services.

Q: Are you open on bank holidays?
A: No. The clinic closes on public holidays and reopens on the next working day.

Q: How far ahead can I book?
A: You can book up to eight weeks ahead. Most patients find something suitable within the next two weeks.

## Booking Appointments

Q: How long is an appointment?
A: Standard appointments are 30 minutes. Let us know when you book if you think you need longer.

Q: Can I choose my doctor?
A: Yes. Tell us who you would like to see and we will show you that doctor's next available times. If you have no preference, we will offer the earliest slots across the whole clinic.

Q: What if I need to be seen urgently?
A: Say that it is urgent when you book and we will offer you the earliest available slot. If it is a medical emergency, hang up and call the emergency services.

Q: Can I book for someone else?
A: You can book on behalf of a family member if they are registered with the clinic. We will ask for their name and date of birth.

Q: Will I get a reminder?
A: Yes. We send a reminder by text and by email the day before your appointment.

## Cancellation Policy

Q: What is the cancellation policy?
A: Please give us at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move an appointment, so we can offer the slot to someone else.

Q: Is there a charge for cancelling late?
A: There is no charge for a late cancellation, but repeated missed appointments without notice may affect how far ahead you can book.

Q: What happens if I miss my appointment?
A: A missed appointment is recorded as a did-not-attend. Call us and we will rebook you at the next suitable time.

Q: How do I move an appointment?
A: Ask to rebook and we will cancel the existing appointment and offer you new times in the same call.

Q: How late can I arrive?
A: If you arrive more than ten minutes late we may not be able to see you, because it pushes every later appointment back.

## What To Bring

Q: What should I bring to my appointment?
A: Bring photo identification, your patient number if you have it, and a list of any medication you are currently taking.

Q: Do I need to bring my medication?
A: Bring the packaging or a written list of what you take, including the dose. That is more reliable than trying to recall it.

Q: Should I bring test results?
A: Yes, bring any results, letters, or scans from other services if the clinic does not already hold them.

Q: Do I need to arrive early?
A: Please arrive five minutes before your appointment time so reception can check you in.

Q: Can someone come with me?
A: Yes, you are welcome to bring one person with you for support or to interpret.

## Fees And Payment

Q: Do I have to pay for an appointment?
A: Standard consultations are covered for registered patients. Some services, such as travel vaccinations and medical certificates, carry a fee.

Q: How can I pay?
A: Payment is taken at reception by card, or by bank transfer if the clinic has invoiced you.

Q: Can I get a receipt for insurance?
A: Yes. Ask at reception on the day and we will issue an itemised receipt.

## Privacy And Your Data

Q: Who can see my medical records?
A: Only the clinicians and administrative staff directly involved in your care can see your records, and every access is logged.

Q: Is this call recorded?
A: Calls with the booking assistant may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Recordings are stored securely and deleted on a fixed schedule.

Q: What do you do with my contact details?
A: We use them to confirm appointments, send reminders, and contact you about your care. We do not sell or share them for marketing.

Q: Can I ask for a copy of my records?
A: Yes. Submit a request to reception and the clinic will respond within one month.

Q: Can I ask you to delete my data?
A: You can ask, but clinics are legally required to keep medical records for a minimum retention period, so some information cannot be deleted on request.

Q: Is it safe to give my details over the phone?
A: Yes. We only ask for what is needed to identify you, and the assistant never reads a full record back out loud.

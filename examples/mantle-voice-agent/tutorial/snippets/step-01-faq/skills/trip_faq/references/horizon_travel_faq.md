# Horizon Travel FAQ

## Baggage
- Economy includes one cabin bag up to 7 kg and one checked bag up to 23 kg.
- Extra checked bags are 45 pounds sterling each way when prepaid online.
- Lithium batteries and aerosols over 100 ml are not allowed in checked bags.

## Check-in
- Online check-in opens 24 hours before departure.
- Airport check-in desks close 45 minutes before short-haul flights and
  60 minutes before long-haul flights.

## Seats
- Standard seat selection is free from 48 hours before departure.
- Extra-legroom seats can be purchased during booking or in Manage Trip.

## Changes and cancellations
- Flexible fares allow one free date change more than 24 hours before departure.
- Non-flexible fares charge a 60 pound change fee plus any fare difference.
- Cancellations within 24 hours of booking are free for UK-originating trips.

## Contact
- Horizon Travel phone support is available daily from 7am to 10pm UK time.
- For irregular operations, Atlas can start a baggage report or booking change.

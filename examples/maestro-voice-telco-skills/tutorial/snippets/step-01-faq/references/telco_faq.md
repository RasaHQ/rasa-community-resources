# Telecom of Rasa — FAQ

## Speed tests

Q: What is an internet speed test?
A: A speed test measures download and upload speeds in megabits per second,
plus ping and jitter. It helps diagnose slow connections and check whether your
line is performing as expected.

Q: How can I run a speed test myself?
A: You can use Fast.com, Speedtest.net, or Google Internet speed test. In our
app, open Home, choose WLAN speed test, and run it.

## Reboot versus reset

Q: What is the difference between rebooting and resetting a router?
A: Rebooting turns the router off and on without changing settings. Resetting
restores factory defaults and erases custom Wi-Fi names, passwords, and advanced
settings. Reboot first for minor issues. Reset only as a last resort.

Q: How do I reboot my router?
A: Press the power button or unplug the power cable. Wait about thirty seconds
to one minute, then turn it back on. This refreshes the connection without
wiping settings.

## Wi-Fi tips

Q: My speed looks fine but the internet still feels slow. What should I try?
A: Move closer to the router, reduce the number of devices streaming at once,
and restart devices on Wi-Fi. Prefer the five gigahertz band for nearby devices
when available.

## Billing

Q: Can Telano explain my bill?
A: Yes. Ask for a specific month. Telano can summarize the amount, compare it
to your average, and list charge sources such as Internet, TV, or Mobile.

Q: Where can I see full bill documents?
A: Open your account bills page in the Telecom of Rasa customer portal, or ask
to be connected to a human specialist for a detailed review.

## Support

Q: When should I speak to a human?
A: If troubleshooting does not restore speed, a bill still looks wrong after a
breakdown, or you need account changes Telano cannot perform, ask for a human
agent.

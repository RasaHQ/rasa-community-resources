## Reservations

Q: What does reserving a car actually do?
A: It places a hold on that specific vehicle at that dealer so nobody else can buy it while you decide.

Q: How long does a reservation last?
A: Reservations are held for three business days. After that the car goes back into general stock.

Q: Does reserving a car cost anything?
A: No. Reservations at Rasa Motors are free and there is no obligation to buy.

Q: Can I reserve more than one car?
A: Yes, but we recommend holding no more than two at a time so other customers can view them.

Q: How do I cancel a reservation?
A: Ask us to cancel it, or tell the dealer when you arrive. There is no cancellation fee.

Q: Can I reserve a car at a different dealer than my nearest one?
A: Yes. Any Rasa Motors dealer can hold a car for you, and transfers between dealers take two to three days.

## Financing

Q: What finance terms do you offer?
A: Rasa Motors Finance offers 36, 48, and 60 month terms at fixed rates.

Q: How is my monthly payment worked out?
A: We take the price, subtract your deposit, then amortise the balance over your chosen term at the advertised rate.

Q: Does a longer term always cost less?
A: The monthly payment is lower, but you pay more total interest over the life of the loan.

Q: Do I need a deposit?
A: A deposit is not required, but every dollar down reduces both the monthly payment and the total interest.

Q: Will you check my credit?
A: Only with your consent. We verify your identity first, then run the check.

Q: Does a credit check affect my score?
A: An initial quote is a soft check and does not affect your score. A formal application is a hard check.

Q: Can I pay cash instead?
A: Yes. Cash buyers skip the finance steps entirely and usually complete paperwork in a single visit.

Q: Can I settle the loan early?
A: Yes, and there is no early settlement penalty on Rasa Motors Finance agreements.

## Dealer appointments

Q: When are the dealers open?
A: Dealers are open Monday to Friday. Appointment slots start at nine, eleven, two, and four.

Q: How long is an appointment?
A: Allow forty five minutes for a test drive or paperwork visit.

Q: Do I need an appointment to visit?
A: Walk-ins are welcome, but booking guarantees a specialist and the car are both ready for you.

Q: What should I bring?
A: Bring a valid driving licence, proof of address, and your part-exchange documents if you have a car to trade in.

Q: Can I change or cancel my appointment?
A: Yes, just tell us the new day and time and we will move it.

## Test drives

Q: Who can take a test drive?
A: Any licenced driver aged twenty one or over with a valid licence they have held for at least twelve months.

Q: How long is a test drive?
A: A standard test drive is around thirty minutes on a mixed route.

Q: Is insurance included on a test drive?
A: Yes, dealer trade insurance covers the drive.

Q: Can I test drive more than one car in a visit?
A: Yes, if both cars are at the same dealer and you book a longer slot.

## Buying and paperwork

Q: Is the advertised price negotiable?
A: Advertised prices are the sale price. Any dealer contribution is agreed at the appointment.

Q: Do you take part exchange?
A: Yes. Bring your current car and its documents to the appointment and the dealer will value it.

Q: What warranty comes with a used car?
A: Used cars include a twelve month warranty. New cars carry the manufacturer warranty.

Q: How soon can I collect the car?
A: Cars at your chosen dealer are usually ready within two working days of completed paperwork.
